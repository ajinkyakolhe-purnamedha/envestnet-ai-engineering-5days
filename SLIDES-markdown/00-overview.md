# The Capstone Project: Chronos Wealth

Fictional money, and a share market you have time-travelled into

- Different shares and their prices, saved in a database, shown via graphs
- Users choose which share to buy and freeze
- Future time-travel of the market, risk analysis, and more
- LLM features layered on top
