# Concentration limit

No single holding may exceed 35% of total portfolio value. An
advisor who observes a position above this limit must raise a
concentration warning in the next client review.

# Cash allocation

Cash above 40% of total portfolio value is flagged as a high cash
allocation. It is not a breach, but the review must record why the
allocation is being held.

# Diversification floor

A portfolio holding a single position is recorded as low
diversification and requires a starting allocation discussion.

# Point-in-time valuation

Every valuation uses the latest close on or before the account's
simulated date. No report, chart, or recommendation may reference a
price after that date.

# Advisor authority

Advisors hold read-only access to investor accounts. An advisor may
analyse a portfolio and draft a review, but may never execute a
trade on a client's behalf.

# Human confirmation

Every proposed action requires human confirmation before execution.
A drafted recommendation is not an instruction.
